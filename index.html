Neck Tightening Procedure in Melville, NY

As skin loses elasticity, the neck can develop looseness, vertical bands and a softer jawline. A neck tightening procedure is designed to address these changes and restore a smoother, firmer contour.

Your plan may combine tightening with liposuction or other contouring techniques, depending on what you need. During your consultation we explain each option, what to expect during recovery and which approach fits your goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a neck tightening procedure?

A neck tightening procedure is a surgical approach that helps smooth loose skin, reduce neck bands and improve the contour between the chin and neck. The exact technique depends on your anatomy, and your surgeon will recommend the best option during consultation.

Who is a good candidate for double chin surgery?

Adults who have stubborn fullness under the chin that does not respond well to diet or exercise are often good candidates. A consultation will confirm whether surgery, or a combined approach with neck contouring, suits you.

How long is recovery after eyelid surgery?

Recovery varies from person to person. Many patients experience swelling and bruising for the first week or two and return to light daily activities fairly soon. Your surgeon will give you personalized aftercare instructions.

Can I combine eyelid, double chin and neck procedures?

In many cases, yes. Procedures for the eyes, chin, jawline and neck can often be planned together for balanced results. We will discuss whether combining them is appropriate for you.
